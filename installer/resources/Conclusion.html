Open your audio host

In Logic Pro, insert Pitch Workbench from Audio FX → Audio Units → Independent Audio Development. If it does not appear, check Logic's Plug-in Manager and rescan the installed Audio Unit.

Other compatible hosts can scan the installed VST3 from their plug-in manager.

Start with a dry vocal. For automatic tuning choose your key and scale. For manual editing capture one pass, stop capture, move a note and replay the same region.

The detailed guide and acceptance checks are in /Library/Application Support/Pitch Workbench/Documentation.
